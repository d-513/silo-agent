---
title: What Silo is
description: A self-hosted agent for several people, where each Bot is a durable machine and the control plane holds every key.
---

Silo is an agent you host yourself. It is built from two kinds of thing.

- A **Bot** is a durable container with a desktop: a browser, a shell, Python, and a workspace of files. It keeps running between conversations and it remembers.
- The **control plane** is one Go program on your server. It runs the agent loop, talks to the model providers, holds every key and token, and decides what each Bot may do.

The split is the point. The model's work happens in a box that holds nothing worth stealing, and everything that could hurt you (a key, a sent message, a public link) passes through a place that checks a rule first.

## What you can do with it

- Talk to a Bot in the console, on your phone, or in Telegram, WhatsApp, Discord and Slack.
- Give it your tools through connectors (MCP servers over HTTP or STDIO, or the library of presets).
- Let it work on its own on a schedule, and read what it did in the Feed and on its Dashboard.
- Share it. A Workspace holds Bots and the people who use them, with roles.
