Delivery performance is back above target after the summer carrier change. The savings came from consolidating two Lyon sites; the remaining risk is peak-season staffing.

On-time delivery rose every month of the quarter. Most of the gain came from the new regional carrier, which now handles 38% of parcels outside the three largest cities.
