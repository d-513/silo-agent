Proposal · Riverside Library

A makerspace in the old reading room

Turn 80 m² that sits empty five days a week into a free workshop with 3D printers, sewing machines and repair tools, open to every library card holder.

The problem

The reading room averages six visitors a day since the study area moved upstairs. Meanwhile the nearest public workshop is 40 minutes away by bus, and the repair café has a waiting list of three months.

What people asked for

  • Somewhere to fix things instead of replacing them
  • Tools nobody owns at home: printers, a laser cutter
  • Weekend sessions for children and teenagers

The plan

Fit out the room with four workbenches, two 3D printers, a small laser cutter and four sewing machines. Volunteers from the repair café run supervised sessions; tools that need training are booked after a 30-minute induction.

Two local firms have offered €20k of the fit-out in exchange for naming the benches.

What we need from you
