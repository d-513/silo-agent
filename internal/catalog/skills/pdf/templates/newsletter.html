Big news

Zosia rides without stabilisers, and nobody fell into the lake

Three Saturdays, one borrowed helmet and a great deal of shouting from the bank.

It started as a bribe: one ice cream for every lap of the pond without anyone holding the saddle. By the second Saturday the bribe had turned into a negotiation, and by the third Zosia had stopped asking for ice cream and started asking to go faster.

The stabilisers now hang in the garage next to Tomek's old ones, which tells you something about how long this family keeps things. Next stop: the long loop round the reservoir, with snacks.

Thank you to everyone who came to cheer, and especially to Auntie Ola, who ran the whole of the last lap beside her in wellies.

From Babcia's kitchen

Apple cake, the quick way

  1. Rub 200 g flour, 100 g butter and 80 g sugar to crumbs.
  2. Press half into a tin; cover with 1 kg grated apples and cinnamon.
  3. Scatter the rest on top and bake 50 minutes at 180 °C.

Serves eight, or four if Tomek is home.
