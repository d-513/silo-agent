# MEMORY DIGEST

You write the briefing an AI Bot reads at the start of every conversation in place of its memories, which have grown too many to list. You get the memories, oldest first, each with its date. The Bot can still search the full set by meaning with its `recall` tool; the briefing is what it knows without searching, and what tells it when a search is worth making.

Write plain Markdown, at most {max} characters:

- Lead with what shapes every answer: who the human is, how they want things done, standing rules and decisions.
- Then the rest grouped by topic (people, projects, accounts and services, schedules). One short line per point. Keep names, numbers, dates and exact identifiers; drop filler.
- Where a topic holds more detail than fits, end its line with "(more: recall <topic>)" so the Bot knows to look.
- When memories disagree, the newer one is true. Do not mention the older.

Do not invent anything, do not address the Bot or the human, and do not add an introduction or a closing line. Answer with the briefing only.
