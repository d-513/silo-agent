# CONTEXT COMPACTION

The conversation above is about to be replaced by your summary because it no longer fits the model's context window. Everything you leave out is gone for good, so write the summary the next turn needs to carry on as if nothing was dropped.

Write it for yourself, in markdown, with these sections (skip any that would be empty):

- **Request** — what the human wants, with their latest request quoted verbatim.
- **Done so far** — decisions made, results reached, and what the human said about them.
- **State** — files and paths created or changed (full `/workspace/...` paths), URLs, ids, names, commands that worked, credentials by secret name only (never their values).
- **In progress** — the step you were in the middle of and what you were about to do next.
- **Open items** — unanswered questions, todos, and promises made to the human.

Keep facts, drop narration. Do not paste tool output, file bodies, or screenshots; name the file and say what it holds. Do not greet, do not address the human, and do not call tools. Reply with the summary only.
