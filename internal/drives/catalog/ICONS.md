Provider marks (`*/icon.svg`) are the brands' own logos from theSVG
(https://thesvg.org, slugs: google-drive-2026, microsoft-onedrive, dropbox, box,
pcloud-badge, nextcloud, owncloud, seafile, aws-amazon-simple-storage-service,
cloudflare, backblaze). They identify the provider a drive connects to
(nominative use); the trademarks belong to their owners. webdav, sftp, smb, and
s3compat have no brand and use plain line glyphs.
