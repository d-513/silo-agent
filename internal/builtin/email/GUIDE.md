Email signs in to your mailbox with an **app password**, not your normal password. The password stays on the Silo server; the Bot never sees it.

- **Gmail:** turn on 2-Step Verification, then create one at myaccount.google.com/apppasswords. IMAP is on by default.
- **Outlook / Microsoft 365:** create an app password under Security → Advanced security options. Work accounts need IMAP and SMTP AUTH enabled by an admin.
- **iCloud:** appleid.apple.com → Sign-In and Security → App-Specific Passwords.
- **Fastmail:** Settings → Privacy & Security → App passwords (IMAP + SMTP access).
- **Yahoo:** Account Security → Generate app password.
- **Other:** pick Other and fill in the IMAP and SMTP servers from your host's docs.

Reading, searching, flagging, and saving attachments are allowed by default. Sending, replying, moving, and deleting ask you first. Change these on the Rules tab.
