# Brand

The source of truth for how Silo looks outside its colors and type (those are in [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md)). The mascot is the **skrzat**, a small farm spirit from Polish folklore: it lives in the granary, works at night for a bowl of porridge, and its hat covers its eyes, so its face is one round nose over a wool beard.

## What is here

| Path | What it is | Edit it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `palette.json` | Ink, paper, the nose, beard and boots, the logo's two greens, the twelve crest fills (hat, detail, coat) in `crest` order, the eight hats in `crest` order | Yes |
| `logo/mark.svg` | The logo: the whole figure in the emerald crest. It is the same on paper and on dark | Yes |
| `logo/mark-small.svg` | The logo redrawn for 24 px and under: head and shoulders, a bigger nose | Yes |
| `logo/mark-mono.svg` | One color only, for stamps and engraving. The beard is knocked out | Yes |
| `logo/lockup.svg`, `logo/stacked.svg` | The mark with the name in outlined Geist 500 | Generated by `wordmark.py` |
| `crests/*.svg` | The eight hats, cobalt and paper by default | Yes |
| `icons/*.svg` | The mark on its grounds: tile, maskable, the three iOS icons, and the favicons, which have no ground so they sit on the browser tab | Generated |
| `sheet.png` | Every hat in every fill, then 28, 20 and 16 px on both grounds | Generated |

## Exporting

`make brand` needs ImageMagick with librsvg and Python 3. It rewrites `icons/`, `sheet.png`, the icons in `web/public`, `web/src/crestArt.ts` (what `Crest.tsx` draws), and on iOS the `AppIcon` set, `silo-mark`, `ios/SiloShared/Crests.xcassets` (every crest at 44 pt and the lock screen's one-color hats, in all three targets) and `Assets.xcassets/Crests` (every crest at 104 pt, the app only). Never edit those by hand.

`wordmark.py` runs only when the name or the mark changes, and needs fonttools (its header says how).

## The crest

A crest is still `color * 8 + shape` (`palette.json` order), so existing Bots keep their number and fill:

| Shape | Was | Hat |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | circle | bobble |
| 1 | blob | bent |
| 2 | squircle | brim |
| 3 | pill | boat |
| 4 | triangle | cone |
| 5 | hex | striped |
| 6 | cloud | toque |
| 7 | drop | stocking |

- `#hat` and `#mittens` take the fill, `#coat` the fill's coat, and a `.detail` stroke its detail color. `#beard`, `#boots` and `#nose` are the same on every Bot in both themes.
- **The felt look is soft shading and a slightly rough edge, nothing more.** Each surface is a radial gradient of three stops (`tones` in `export.py`) and the `felt` filter roughens the edge. Fiber noise, glossy highlights, fold lines and beard strands were all tried: they look dirty at crest sizes and like veins at logo size.
- The eight files share one body; only `#hat` differs. Mist takes a `mist_edge` outline, and the ink crest an edge in the dark, as DESIGN.md says.
- Keep the bottom right clear: the status lamp sits there.

## Rules

- **If it stands for a Bot it is vector. If it stands for Silo it can be felt.** Felt renders are for ads, the landing page, the stores, sign-in and first run, never a working page.
- The name is *skrzat*, in every language. It is the mascot's name; the product word stays **Bot**.
- **Always the whole figure.** A hat over a nose alone is a mushroom: the beard, coat and boots are what make it a creature. Only `mark-small.svg` crops, and it keeps the beard and coat.
- No Christmas: no red cone with a white beard, no snow.
- No bell shapes: in an app a bell means notifications.
- No beige fuzz, face windows, dot eyes or blush. No horizontal slit on a dome: dark, it reads as a niqab.
