# Brand

The source of truth for how Silo looks outside its colors and type (those are in [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md)). The character is the **Stack**: a small head with a face, sitting on a pile of rounded layers. Every Bot is shown as one, and the same character at rest (the `wobble` shape in `green`) is Silo's mark.

## What is here

| Path | What it is | Edit it? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `tokens.json` | The eight hues (five stops each), the eight shapes (their boxes on a 100 x 100 grid, corners, eyes), the default roster, the badge and the one-color rule | Yes |
| `reference/` | The designer's drawings: every roster Bot in every state at 100 px (`bots/`), the mark (`mark/`) and the mark at 40, 24 and 16 px (`small/`). `web/src/stack_test.ts` holds the renderer to them byte for byte | Only with a new handoff |
| `render.ts` | Draws what `export.py` asks for with the web's renderer, `web/src/stack.ts` | Yes |
| `logo/mark.svg`, `glyph.svg`, `mark-mono.svg`, `mark-small.svg` | The mark on its tile, without it, in one color, and as drawn at 16 px | Generated |
| `logo/lockup.svg`, `logo/stacked.svg` | The mark with the name | Generated by `wordmark.py` |
| `icons/*.svg` | The app icons (full bleed, for the stores to cut), the maskable icon and the favicons | Generated |
| `sheet.png` | Every shape in every hue, the roster in every state, then 40, 24 and 16 px on both grounds | Generated |

## Exporting

`make brand` needs ImageMagick with librsvg, Python 3 and Node 22.6 or newer. It rewrites `logo/` (but not the lockups), `icons/`, `sheet.png`, the icons in `web/public`, `web/src/crestArt.ts` (the tokens as the web reads them), `ios/SiloClient/Sources/SiloClient/Logic/StackArt.swift` (the same for iOS) and on iOS the `AppIcon` set and `silo-mark`. Never edit those by hand. After changing `tokens.json`, run it and then the tests that pin the drawing: `node --experimental-strip-types web/src/stack_test.ts`, and `swift test` in `ios/SiloClient`.

The geometry itself (what a state does to a shape, what a size leaves out) is code, not tokens: `web/src/stack.ts`, ported to `ios/SiloClient/Sources/SiloClient/Logic/Stack.swift`. A change to one is a change to the other.

`wordmark.py` runs only when the name or the mark changes, and needs fonttools (its header says how). Run `make brand` again after it, so `web/public` gets the new lockups.

## The crest

A Bot stores its look as one number, `hue * 8 + shape`, in the order of `tokens.json`:

| # | Shape | What makes it recognizable | Eyes at rest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | wobble | Two layers, each nudged sideways | dots, smile |
| 1 | duo | One tall layer under a tall head | happy arcs, smile |
| 2 | pyramid | Widths grow toward the bottom | dots, smile |
| 3 | tower | Three thin layers | wide eyes |
| 4 | blocks | Square corners, no offset | dashes |
| 5 | hourglass | Narrow middle layer | dots, smile |
| 6 | tilt | Head tilted 9 degrees, small base | wink |
| 7 | coins | Three thin layers, staggered | happy arcs |

The hues, in order: green, pink, amber, blue, gray, violet, sky, lime. Never reorder either list: only restyle.

- **Anatomy:** the ground is a rounded tile in the hue's deepest color (corner 24% of the tile), which is also the ink of the eyes. The head is the lightest stop and carries the face. One layer takes stop `b`; two take `b, c`; three take `a, b, c`. Pieces are pills, except `blocks` (radius 4), with a gap of 2.
- **States** move the pieces and change the eyes: idle, listening, working, needs you (badge "!"), done (badge with a check), asleep (two z's). DESIGN.md § Crests says what each means in the product and how it moves.
- **Sizes:** full detail from 76 px; no smile and bigger eyes under 76; a tighter square (`10 10 80 80`) and no badge under 48, where the app overlays its own on the top right corner; the resting face only, centred, under 20. 16 px is the smallest.
- On a dark page the gray tile takes a 1 px edge, or it disappears.
- Before the Stack a crest was one of eight hats in one of twelve fills. `db.Migrate` moved every stored crest once: the shape number is kept and the fill goes to its nearest hue.

## The mark

- The mark is the first roster Bot at rest: `logo/mark.svg`. On its own surface use `logo/glyph.svg`.
- App icon: the mark full bleed on `#123B33`. iOS's dark icon is the same; its tinted icon is the one-color mark on black.
- One color: `logo/mark-mono.svg`. The head at full opacity, the layers at 68% and 40% (55% for one layer; 76%, 58% and 40% for three).
- Favicons carry the tile, drawn by the size rules: `favicon.svg` as at 32 px, `favicon-small.svg` as at 16.
- Lockup: the tile on the left, "silo" in lowercase on the right, centred on the tile; the gap is a fifth of the tile and the type four fifths of it. Stacked: the name under the tile.
- **The wordmark's typeface is not final.** The designer's mockups used Unbounded SemiBold as a placeholder that must not ship without sign-off, so the lockups are set in the app's own Geist 600 until the owner picks one.

## Rules

- The hue is decoration. Bots are told apart by shape and by name, and a state is never shown by the crest alone: a word says it too.
- Flat color only: no gradients, shading, outlines or textures on a Stack.
- Any shape takes any hue. Do not hand-draw a state for one Bot: `layout()` in `stack.ts` places every shape in every state.
- The product word stays **Bot**; "Stack" is the name of the character, for people working on it.
